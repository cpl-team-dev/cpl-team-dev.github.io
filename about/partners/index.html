Our Partners

We’re funded entirely by our partners and your donations. Here are some of the organisations who help make our work possible.

Southampton City Council

Southampton City Council is the local authority of the city of Southampton. It provides a full range of local government services including libraries, social services, processing planning applications and more, and it is a local education authority.

The Garfield Weston Foundation

The Garfield Weston Foundation is a family-founded, charitable grant-making foundation, which supports a wide range of causes across the UK, donating over £70 million annually. It was established in 1958 by the Weston family and is one of the largest and most respected charitable institutions in the UK which has donated over £1 billion in total. The Trustees are descendants of the founder and the Weston family continues to take a highly active and hands-on approach.

Almost 2000 charities across the UK benefit each year from grants made by the Foundation, ranging from small community and volunteer projects to large national organisations. Despite this range, the common theme is that charities demonstrate they are meeting a need effectively with clear outcomes and benefits, good leadership, sensible business plans and a commitment to excellence.

Richard Kirkman Trust

The Richard Kirkman Trust is a UK grant-making charity (registered charity no. 327972) that supports organisations and individuals addressing social needs across Hampshire, Dorset, the Isle of Wight, Portsmouth, and Southampton.

Southampton Solent Rotary

Southampton Solent Rotary was born out of Southampton Rotary Club, Southampton West Rotary Club and Southampton Clausentum Rotary Club in 2022. They care about education and literacy, the environment, disease prevention and control, and water sanitation locally and globally.

Eastleigh Masons

Eastleigh Masons is a local Masonic lodge in Eastleigh, Hampshire. They are a charitable organisation that supports local charities.
