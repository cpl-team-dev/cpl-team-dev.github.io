Toddler Groups in Southampton

› Services › Toddler Groups

Who?

Toddler Groups are for families with children under five years of age; they are held in a variety of community based venues across the city.

If you are looking for toddler groups in Southampton or a baby and toddler group in Southampton with free or low-cost options, this is a great place to start.

Why?

Toddler Groups offer somewhere to meet new people and make friendships, socialise with people from other backgrounds and to have fun. They are ideally run by everyone for everyone but there should be a named person for contact who may lead on the group.

How can I find one?

Check out your nearest Toddler Group using the Southampton City Council Directory, where you can find detailed information about groups in your area including times, addresses, and contact details. For details of other groups please contact the Southampton Family Information Service.

This can also help if you are specifically looking for a playgroup in Swaythling or a playgroup elsewhere in Southampton.

Tips

  • Visit a few groups for variety.
  • Look for a safe and stimulating environment for children to develop in and opportunities to explore a variety of activities whilst encouraging play together.

Good to know

  • Most groups charge a small fee which covers rent, snacks, insurance, and activity material costs etc.
  • Each group should have policies and procedures including Welcome Information; make sure you ask about these when visiting groups.
  • Parents and carers must be responsible for their own children whilst attending a group.
