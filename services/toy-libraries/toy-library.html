Toy Library

Free toy loans, friendly advice, and a growing catalogue for families and groups across Southampton.

Home > Services > Toy Library
Free to borrow

Browse toys, puzzles, role play and more

Our toy library at Swaythling Neighbourhood Centre is free for families to use, and we also support smaller toy libraries around Southampton. If something catches your eye, let us know the toy name or code and we can help with the next step.

This page shows a curated sample from the current toy index while we continue moving the wider catalogue across to the static site.

How it works

  1. 1. Browse the sample below and make a note of the toy name or stock code.
  2. 2. Email or call us if you want to check availability before you travel.
  3. 3. Our team will talk you through collection, return times and any toy-library questions.
