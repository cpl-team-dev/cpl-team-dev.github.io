Free borrowing

Toy Libraries

We operate a completely free toy library at our main base in Swaythling Neighbourhood Centre, and support a number of smaller toy libraries around Southampton.

Find out more

Weekly sessions

Playtime

Every Tuesday, we run free playtime sessions for children under five and their families.

With toys, crafts, activities, songs and stories, Playtime gives children a fun place to explore while parents and carers connect with others.

Find out more

Special occasions

Soft Play & Party Hire

Reserve a low-cost soft play kit for little guests, or build a toy bundle for a party that feels completely your own.
