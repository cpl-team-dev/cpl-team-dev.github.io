Welcome to our new Assistant Toy Librarian!

› Noticeboard › Welcome to our new Assistant Toy Librarian!

Community Playlink Admin · 1 February 2024 · Staff Updates

Our new Assistant Toy Librarian, Joyce Hamilton-Dyer, has joined the Community Playlink family. You can pop in to meet her on Thursdays and Saturdays every week! Joyce has been using the Toy Library for the last 4 years with her family, and many of you will already have met her through our Playtime sessions. We are very pleased that she has joined our Team—please pop in to see her and say hello!
