Volunteering

Why volunteer with us?

As a small, local charity, volunteers are extremely important to keep Community Playlink running.

Volunteering with us can also be a brilliant opportunity to get work experience if you're studying to work with children, or if you need volunteering as part of a programme like the Duke of Edinburgh award.

What can I help with?

Some of our volunteer responsibilities include:

  • Helping with the toy library
  • General office duties
  • Checking and cleaning toys
  • Cataloguing toys
  • DIY, e.g. mending wooden toys
