Community Playlink is a registered charity operating free toy libraries for families with children under 14. We also support toddler groups and early years settings in Southampton and the surrounding areas.

We also run weekly playtime sessions for families with children under 5 years of age. Come and join in these fun sessions at our base in Swaythling Neighbourhood Centre!

If you are searching for a free toy library in Southampton, toddler groups in Southampton, or affordable party hire in Southampton, Community Playlink is here to help as Southampton’s children’s charity toy library.
