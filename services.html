Toy Libraries

We operate a completely free toy library at our main base in Swaythling Neighbourhood Centre, and support a number of smaller toy libraries around Southampton.

Playtime

Every Tuesday, we run free playtime sessions for children under five and their families. With toys, crafts, activities, songs, and stories, it'll be your child's favourite part of the week.

Party Hire

Having a party? Make it one to remember by hiring toys and soft play from our extensive toy library for a brilliant price.
